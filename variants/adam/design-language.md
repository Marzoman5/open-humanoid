# Adam design language

Inherits [docs/design-language.md](../../docs/design-language.md) (frame + shell, sleek, not toy-like).

## Adam-specific direction (draft, to be confirmed by maintainer, T-0003)
- **Proportions:** slim adult, long legs (legs are 50% of height), narrow waist, head 14% of height.
- **Shell:** matte off-white main shells with dark graphite joint recesses and face visor. One accent: a thin light line on the visor.
- **Silhouette:** continuous thigh-to-shank line. Servo bodies sit fully inside the thigh/shank volume (STS3215 is about 45×25×35 mm, so segment cross-sections must clear about 30×50 mm plus walls).
- **Face:** smooth, featureless visor. Camera (later) behind a tinted window.
