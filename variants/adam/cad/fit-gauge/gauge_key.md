## Record the result

Fill in `gauge_results.yaml` (see `read_results` in openhumanoid/cad/gauge.py) with the index (1 = first hole) that fits:

```yaml
servo_slice_drops_in: true            # G1, snug but no force
servo_height_fits: true               # G2 (35 mm axis)
horn_hole_radius_mm: 7.0               # G3, where the horn screw holes line up in the slots
horn_od_mm: 20.0
insert_m3_hole_index: 4
bearing_seat_index: 3
clearance_slip_index: 3     # G6, smallest hole where the peg slides by hand
clearance_rotating_index: 6
magnet_pocket_index: 2
```

Items marked ESTIMATE in the catalog (`estimates_to_confirm` in gauge.json) are what G1-G3 verify: the horn axis offset, boss height, horn bolt circle, back-shaft and cable-exit positions. Measure them with calipers while the servo is out.
