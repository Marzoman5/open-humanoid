# STS3215 notes (accessed 2026-10-19; re-verified against sources, see research/verification-parts/feetech.md)

**Voltage variants (resolved).** 19.5 kg.cm is the 7.4 V figure (Feetech product page "Stall Torque 19.5kg.cm@7.4V"; Waveshare 7.4V SKU 33014 "19.5kg.cm@7.4V", 2.5 A locked rotor). At 6 V it is 16.5 kg.cm (servodatabase, switch-science). Evelta/rcdrone/core-electronics print 19.5 "@6V": a retailer label error, ignore. 30 kg.cm is the 12 V variant (C018/C047): Waveshare "30kg.cm@12V", Evelta, Seeed C047; 2.7 A locked rotor, 45 rpm, kt 11 kg.cm/A. These are different motor builds (kt 7.8 vs 11 kg.cm/A), not one servo run at two voltages. Both are 1:345 (Feetech/Seeed/openelab); C044 = 1:191 and C046 = 1:147 (7.4 V, SO-ARM101 leader; horndeer README) have no sourced torque/speed.

**Can a 3S LiPo (12.6 V) power a 7.4 V variant?** Rated range of the 7.4 V part is 4-7.4 V (Waveshare) or 6-7.4 V (Evelta, rcdrone, core-electronics, switch-science). 12.6 V is 70% over. No damage report found, but no evidence of tolerance either: treat as out of spec. The 12 V part's range is 6-12.6 V (Waveshare) / 4-14 V (Evelta), so a full 3S pack (12.6 V) is inside rating. Firmware Max Voltage Limit register default is reportedly 14.0 V on at least one unit; this is firmware, not a hardware rating.

**Rated vs stall.** Retailer "rated" torque: 6.5 (Evelta, 6 V label) or 5 (Seeed C001) vs 19.5 stall on the 7.4 V part; 10 vs 30 kg.cm at 12 V (Evelta, Seeed C047). So vendor-rated is 26-33% of stall. The "continuous <=50% of max" advice (rcdrone) could NOT be verified (page returns 403; other language pages lack it). Independent evidence at 12 V (Robonine, one C018 unit): 15 kg.cm (50% of 30 stall) held 48 C for >1 h static and +15 C in 10 min of motion, but a +-90 deg oscillation at 15 kg.cm reached 71 C after ~110 min; 20 kg.cm tripped overload after several cycles. No measurement for the 7.4 V part.

**Protection.** Registers (Feetech "EPROM" 5-39): Max_Temperature_Limit(13)=70 C; Overload_Torque(36)=80 %; Protection_Time(35)=200 (unit unconfirmed; 2 s if 10 ms); Protective_Torque(34)=20 % while tripped. Robonine saw torque cut to ~20 % under prolonged load, and an overheat flag at 70 C without active shutdown (71 C reached). Protection_Current(28) default conflicts between sources (511 vs 0); do not rely on it. Prior claims "clears on new position command" and "overcurrent ~2 A for 2 s" are unsupported.

**Spec conflicts that remain.** Stall current 2.0 A (6 V), 2.5 A (7.4 V, Waveshare) - servodatabase's 2-2.7 A range mixes variants. Motor type (coreless vs core) and gear material (copper/steel/metal) differ by listing. Backlash: Robonine measured 0.87 deg on one C018 (datasheet claim <=0.5 deg per Robonine); servodatabase's 0.870 has unknown provenance. C001 backlash: not measured.

**Gear/MOSFET failures (anecdotal).** HomeBrew Robotics Club thread, 16 Aug 2026: one poster saw gears strip on stalls above ~15 % of max torque limit (he states ~4.5 kg.cm, i.e. 30 kg.cm basis; voltage not stated), burned out 3 servos; another says he would blow the internal MOSFETs before stripping gears and advises greatly reducing supply voltage during software development [thread](https://groups.google.com/g/hbrobotics/c/wMPp7it97i4). Two posts, unverified, SKU/voltage not given.

**Registers / PID.** Addresses match LeRobot v0.4.0 (P=21, D=22, I=23, Torque_Enable=40, Acceleration=41, Goal_Position=42, Lock=55, Present_Position=56, Load=60, Voltage=62, Temp=63, Current=69; byte order not verified). Factory P=32, D=32, I=0 (commanderfun register ref; tomwhipple measured). LeRobot overwrites to P=16, I=0, D=32. Baud codes (LeRobot): 0=1M, 1=500k, 2=250k, 3=128k, 4=115.2k, 5=57.6k, 6=38.4k, 7=19.2k. Torque-enable 128 = calibrate midpoint and the sign-bit handling: NOT verified. Max servos per bus: null.

**Sim-model pitfall.** Popular MJCF has kp 998 / kv 2.7 / +-2.94 vs upstream kp 17.8; stiff gains saturate [blog](https://mcobzarenco-fontaine-blog.static.hf.space/posts/2026-08-11-sim-servo-sysid.html). Real servo lags commands by a mean 2.19 deg at 30 Hz. The fitted forcerange +-3.478 N.m is an effective sim parameter, above any datasheet stall (35.5 kg.cm), voltage unknown.

**Counterfeits/QC.** Searched; no source documenting counterfeit STS3215 or batch QC found (null). Only evidence: listings disagree on motor type and gear material. Verify with register readout (model number reg) and buy from Feetech distributors.

**Not found:** voltage-sag behavior, per-variant 1:191/1:147 speed/current/torque, C001 backlash, any 12V-motor-on-3S dynamic test, BAM 12 V identification.
