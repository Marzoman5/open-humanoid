## Gotchas / decisions made
- **`pip install sdf` is NOT fogleman's SDF library.** It's a "Scientific Data Format" package. Use manifold3d `level_set` with numpy SDF helpers.
- `trimesh` was pip-installed into the shared main-repo `.venv`. manifold3d and pyvista were installed only in a scratch dir.
- `bpy` 5.0.1 has a cp311 wheel (resolves in our venv). bpy 5.2.x needs Python 3.13.
- A per-point Python SDF callback in manifold3d `level_set` took 120 s. Vectorise it.
- The worktree has no `.venv`. Run `oh` via `../../../.venv/Scripts/python -m openhumanoid` from the worktree root, which picks up the worktree's package.
- Proportion bands (06 §7) are anthropometric starting points that haven't been verified. The Winter/Drillis–Contini table wasn't confirmed online.
- No humanoid company has published its industrial-design process. The ID-process section rests on general CAD/ID sources.
