## Gotchas / decisions made
- **Calibrated gate set.** Computed beside the strict one in `variants/adam/design/r1_calibrated.py`; thresholds were not touched.
  - RMS ×1.94 is applied against 0.637 N·m for the 7.4 V part and against the catalog rated torque otherwise.
  - Overload, validity and R10 are NOT relaxed.
  - The 0.45 m-table pickup is excluded from validity, the same rule as `sweep.relaxed_feasibility`.
- **The engine mirrors every joint.** One-gripper builds were modelled with two grippers (conservative mass); asymmetric arms are not modelled.
- **Generated files.** `generated/concept/R1-P1b-0p55-3s-sts3250-knee/` is an evaluated alternative (STS3250 at the knees only). The harness blocks deleting generated dirs, so it stays.
- **Packaging script choices** (challenge them): hip-yaw servos run fore-aft so the waist-yaw servo fits between them; the Pi 5 bay is in the rear pelvis; the 3S 1300 pack is across the upper back; the arm cant is the minimum that clears the hips; section width is 1.12 × the box.
- **Harness path warnings.** The PreToolUse hook prints "outside the paths claimed" warnings for files that ARE inside the claimed paths: it drops the backslashes, giving `C:Users/...`. They are cosmetic, but they appear on every Bash write. Worth a LESSONS row and a hook fix.
- **Windows Bash safety checks.** They refuse loops over variables, `cd`, or `$VAR` before python or git. Write helper scripts with the Write tool and pass literal paths.
