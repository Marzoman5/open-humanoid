## Gotchas / decisions made
- Policy is strict by design: walking checks include an endurance check (PETG 10 % of UTS is an ASSUMPTION borrowed from PLA, CF-nylon 17 % borrowed from neat Nylon 12). Coupons (docs/strength.md section 9) replace it; margins scale directly.
- Fall loads on the structure are limited by servo assumptions (side force 150 N, side moment 1 x stall, torque 2 x stall) and by the `fall.exposed_links` assumption (hip links inside the pelvis shell). The unprotected rigid-contact case (1691 N) fails everything and is informational only.
- leg_v3 costs hip pitch range: extension 40 -> 33 deg, flexion -110 -> -108 deg (walking needs about -46..+25, squat -88). Tell the concept loop if +40 is a MUST.
