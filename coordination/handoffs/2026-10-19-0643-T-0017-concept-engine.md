## Gotchas / decisions made
- The 12 V STS3215 uses the datasheet (stall 2.94 N.m, 4.78 rad/s) with 7.4 V dynamics borrowed; `servo_spec("sts3215-12v", "bam_scaled")` keeps the brief's "kt same" scaling as an optimistic bound (speed 9.2 rad/s contradicts the 45 rpm datasheet/Robonine).
- Peak gate uses a 100 ms moving average of the demand (touchdown spikes of a stiff ideal replay are not transmitted by a real servo-foot system); assumption, in thresholds.yaml.
- Upstream BAM needs Python >= 3.12 and is not on PyPI as `bam` (package name `better-actuator-models`); the fidelity test needs `BAM_SRC=<checkout>` and is skipped otherwise. Result: identical to 1e-16.
- Heredocs in this harness are fragile: write scripts to files.
- `variants/adam/generated/concept/` holds tool-written reports; regenerate with `python -m openhumanoid.concept evaluate <file>` (about 5 min each).
