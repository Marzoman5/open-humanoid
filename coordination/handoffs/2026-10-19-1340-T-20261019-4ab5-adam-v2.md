## Linkage findings from the analysis (to verify in CAD; nothing built yet)

1. **The coupler plane is set by the servo's horn stack, not by the slim joint.** The input crank sits on the horn: face + 2 boss + 0.3 float + 3 horn + 4.3 plate (+5.6 at a bearing boss). The coupler must therefore run at about 24–34 mm from the leg centre plane. It cannot pass medially inside the horn stack, because its pin sweeps over the servo body.
   - **Consequence:** the realistic front-view knee is about 40–48 mm wide, not the 32 mm the packaging study assumed (R1 fork: 52 mm). The P8a gain (0.956 → 0.623) is therefore overstated.
   - **To do:** re-measure the knee and ankle envelopes in CAD, then re-run the look metrics with the measured keep-outs.
   - Best layout found: servo face at about +13 mm, so the cover stays at the R1 medial limit of −26.5 mm. The knee pin clevis is offset laterally (the joint position along y does not change the kinematics).
2. **Crank angle window.** θ = θ0 + q, with the dead points at 0° and 180°.
   - Knee: θ0 = 35°, giving θ ∈ [35°, 145°] over 0–110° of knee flexion.
   - Ankle: θ0 ≈ 97.5°, giving θ ∈ [37.5°, 122.5°] over the engine range [−60°, +25°]. Sign convention: negative = dorsiflexion (CAD rotation about +y).
   - Coupler force: F = T / (r·sin θ).
3. **Loads at stall.**
   - Knee, STS3250 4.9 N·m, r = 22 mm, θ = 35°: about 390 N.
   - Ankle, STS3215 2.94 N·m, r = 18 mm: about 285 N.
   - **623ZZ C0r = 218 N (NSK, via web search 2026-10-19).** One bearing per pin carries the walking loads but exceeds C0 at stall at the range ends. Plan: 2× 623ZZ per pin (boss 9.6 mm; it can protrude medially about 4 mm under the crank plate, beyond the horn radius).
   - M3 12.9 SHCS clamping the coupler and the inner race: shear about 80–110 MPa, OK. An unclamped M3 used as a cantilever pin fails in bending.
4. **Coupler.** A printed coupler fails buckling (PETG E = 1.68 GPa: about 40 N out of plane at 3 mm thick, about 770 N at 10 × 8 mm).
   - Use 6061-T6 flat bar, 3 × 10 mm: out-of-plane Euler load about 1.66 kN at about 92 mm length.
   - Stock: 1/8" × 3/8" not found; 1/8" × 3" × 12" is $5.31 at Speedy Metals (cut strips).
5. **Play model to implement.** Output angular play = (δ_pin1 + δ_pin2) / (r·sin θ), plus the servo backlash (0.5° datasheet; 0.87° measured on one unit).
   - Pin play cases: best about 0.02 mm (clamped, bearing clearance only); nominal about 0.08 mm; worst about 0.24 mm (loose press seat 0.1 + M3 in a 3.0 bore 0.13).
   - The backlash goes into the engine through `ServoSpec.backlash_rad`, per joint, in a variant-local wrapper (like packaging/engine.py). It is a servo-in-the-loop sensitivity only; it never enters a gate.
6. **Layout bug to fix.** The packaging layout puts the knee servo top 1 mm below the hip-pitch housing (`zc = hip_bot − MOVE − hs`). That ignores the hip-pitch sweep (leg_v2 needed a 24 mm web radius).
   - Move the knee servo axis down to about z = 235 at 0.55 m: rod about 86.5 mm, still long enough.
   - Include the hip-pitch housing as a static body in the ROM sweep.
