- Reachable table tops (object centre 50 mm behind the edge, scan floor 0.15 m): R1-P1r 0.55 m: 0.150-0.159 and 0.206-0.384 m; R1-P1 0.60 m: 0.150-0.163 and 0.191-0.422 m.

Servo-in-the-loop grasp lifts the object (26-32 mm) everywhere it was run. The 131x spike is gone.
- R5 at a 0.40-0.45 m table is NOT established: P1r reaches 0.40 only with the object within ~35 mm of the edge and 0.45 only at the edge; P1 reaches 0.40-0.42 by default and 0.45 with the object within ~35 mm of the edge. Cause: arm geometry (shoulder height table, forearm meets the edge), not balance or legs.

## Gotchas / decisions made
- Collision check is against EVERY robot geom (the MJCF only collides fingers/feet), capsule links, 3 mm margin; real servo housings are bigger, re-check in CAD.
- Assumptions that move the answer: object 50 mm behind the edge (`edge_to_object_m`), table slab 0.30 x 0.40 m, finger tilt <= 45 deg, arm >= 4 mm short of straight.
- The pelvis moves back ~1 cm when the arm extends (COM compensation); the planner re-anchors the waypoints on the grasp-time shoulder.
- Planner search budgets (coarse then fine pass) mean heights out of reach by a hair are reported unreachable; the low-table gap (0.16-0.20 m) is thighs/knees vs the table edge.
- Default evaluation still FAILs on `pickup_0.45m` invalid (R1 concepts), same as before, with a clearer reason.
