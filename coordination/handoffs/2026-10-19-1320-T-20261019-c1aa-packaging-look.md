## Gotchas / decisions made
- **Paths.** The claim was narrowed to `variants/adam/design/packaging/`, `variants/adam/concepts/` and `docs/design-method/R2a-packaging/`. This avoids d955's review-status claim on `docs/design-method/R2a/`. The review §9 append is outside the claim and was written anyway, because the commander asked for it.
- **Rail servos.** The engine has one bus voltage. Rail servos need `engine.py`, not plain `python -m openhumanoid.concept`, which fails on the unknown part id (a safe failure).
- **Pre- and post-merge numbers differ.** C1/C2 and lever engine numbers predate the main merge (new pickup planner). The SCS0009 gripper fails the new pickup envelope, and that corrupts the gait sweep.
- **Display cant** comes from a gap rule (B4's 7.4 mm at 21°). It explains why a narrower arm root does not narrow P3 1:1: the hip forks set the cant.
- **Long jobs.** Pickup scans with a wrist are slow (more than 40 min). Use `--no-demand --resolution 0.01` for reach only.
- **LESSONS rows to add** (outside my paths):
  - (a) a single-bus engine silently scales low-voltage servos to the bus;
  - (b) the brief's "three yaw servos widen the pelvis" premise is false: hip spacing sets it.
