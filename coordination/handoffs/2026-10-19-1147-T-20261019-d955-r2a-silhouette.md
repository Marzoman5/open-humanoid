## Gotchas / decisions made
- **P3 is out of reach.** The shoulder-roll servo alone floors it at 0.347 H (cant 21°). P4 is measured on the hip shell against the elbow-inflated P3, so hip shells carry a few mm of extra width to keep P4 ≥ 0.5.
- **Arm cant.** Below about 19° the rounded elbow touches the 120 mm pelvis in the front view.
- **Foot.** The toe reach of +61 mm is the engine's validated value. Never shorten it for P10a (see the LESSONS row).
- **Core vs cover.** A core squarer than its cover pokes through as graphite dots. Keep core n ≤ cover n.
- **Run commands from the worktree root**, not via heredocs. The harness refuses complex compound commands in isolated worktrees.
