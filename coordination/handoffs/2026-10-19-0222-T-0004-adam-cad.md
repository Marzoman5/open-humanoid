## Packaging lessons (STS3215, 45.2 × 24.7 × 35 mm, at h = 0.50 m)
- **Any joint axis forces 35 mm of servo across it.** Add the horn (3 mm) and two yoke plates (2.5 mm each) and the yoke is 46 mm wide. With shell gap and wall, a pitch-joint limb section is about 52–64 × 45 mm. The thigh shell came out at 46 × 53 mm and the shank at 47 × 70 mm, because the knee yoke wraps outside the servo. That's wider than the thigh, the opposite of the design language. A single-sided yoke would save about 6 mm.
- **Intersecting-axis gimbals fit** (hip yaw/roll/pitch, ankle pitch/roll) and match the sim's coincident joints. The cost is stand-offs: each gimbal needs about 24 mm (corner radius 16.7 + clearance + wall + horn) between the axis and the next servo's horn face. The hip-roll servo sits 24–59 mm behind the hip (glute), and the ankle-roll servo sits 24–59 mm ahead of the ankle (toe cap).
- **The foot grows** from 85 × 38 mm (spec) to 92 × 55 mm. Ankle height 22.5 mm leaves only about 4.6 mm under the ankle-roll servo. Ankle roll is estimated at about ±18°, not ±30°: the yoke hits the sole. Raising `ankle_height` to about 0.06 fixes it.
- **Joint "bands" are servo-sized** (25–45 mm), because shells must stop at yoke sweep radii (hip about 25 mm, knee/elbow about 20 mm). The design-language goal of 2–3 mm bands isn't achievable with exposed U-yokes.
- **Arms vs body:** with `shoulder_width` 0.30, straight arms hit the thighs and hip caps. It needs a 10° A-pose cant or `shoulder_width` of about 0.36. The two shoulder servos fill the chest width (4 mm gap left with 7.5 mm horn spacers).
- **Hips** are 143 mm wide with the two yaw servos lying across the pelvis. That's the minimum `hip_width` of about 2·(L − offset) + 2 = 70 mm between hip centres.
- **Mass:** the CAD printed structure is 0.59 kg vs the sizing model's 0.80 kg, so `structure_kg_at_1m` would calibrate from 6.4 to about 4.73. With that, sizing finds 0.55 m feasible at $399.42 (prices unverified, no detail features yet).
- The 3S 2200 battery (est. 105 × 34 × 24) only fits lying across the upper back. Electronics need catalog `dimensions_mm` (task T-0011).
