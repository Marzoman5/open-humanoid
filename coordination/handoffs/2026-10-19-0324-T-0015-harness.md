## Gotchas / decisions made
- Commander gets **ask**, not deny, on contracts/gate-config. Workers must list the file in their task `paths` (an ADR task). This is the escape hatch that keeps the commander and humans unlocked.
- `generated/` is denied for everyone, but `git checkout -- <generated path>` is fine (hooks only look at what a command writes).
- Gate records: "at HEAD" tolerates later commits that only add gate records or touch `coordination/`; otherwise STALE. Workflow: commit, `oh gate`, commit the record. `is_dirty` ignores `generated/` and `coordination/`.
